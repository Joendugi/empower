## 1. Acceptance of Terms
By creating an account or accessing learning materials on Empower, learners, educators, and institutional partners agree to these terms and the associated Data Privacy and Security Policies.

## 2. Learner Accounts & Conduct
* Learners agree to provide accurate information and maintain the confidentiality of their credentials.
* Practical evidence uploaded (photos, audio, video) must represent the learner's own authentic workshop and lab work.
* Submitting plagiarised or fraudulent evidence may result in revocation of certified badges and account suspension.

## 3. Educator & Curriculum Author Responsibilities
* Educators submitting course modules warrant that curriculum materials adhere to TVET CDACC / NITA occupational guidelines and respect intellectual property rights.
* Content submitted enters an administrative quality assurance queue before public dissemination.

## 4. Certification & Credential Verification
* Empower provides verified digital skills transcripts and badges based on completed module assessments.
* Official national accreditation remains subject to evaluation by authorized vocational bodies.

## 5. Service Availability & Offline Operations
* Empower provides offline caching to support continuous learning in low-connectivity areas. Synchronised progress updates are uploaded once an internet connection is re-established.
