# Data Privacy & Protection Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Applicability:** Empower / CyberLearn Platform (Web, PWA, API & Offline Synchronisation Engines)  
**Regulatory Alignment:** Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA) 2019, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR principles), and TVET CDACC Standards.

---

## 1. Principles of Data Processing

Empower is committed to processing learner, educator, and institutional data in accordance with the following core principles:
1. **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**: Personal data is collected only with informed consent and for explicit vocational education purposes.
2. **Purpose Limitation**: Data is collected strictly for account management, course progression tracking, assessment verification, and certification.
3. **Data Minimisation**: Only necessary identifiers (display name, email, preferred language, and learning progress records) are collected.
4. **Accuracy & Portability**: Learners maintain the right to review, export, and correct their skills transcripts at any time.
5. **Storage Limitation**: Offline device caches and inactive accounts are managed with defined retention windows.
6. **Integrity & Confidentiality**: Technical and organisational safeguards prevent unauthorised access, modification, or data loss.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Learner Information
* **Account Credentials**: Name/pseudonym, email address, password (stored as cryptographic hashes only).
* **Learning Metrics**: Lessons completed, exercise scores, XP earned, streaks, and assessment timestamps.
* **Practical Workshop Evidence**: Photos, audio, and video recordings submitted for practical competency grading.

### B. Educator & Institutional Information
* **Professional Credentials**: Institution name, trainer accreditation reference, submitted curriculum drafts, and review logs.

### C. Technical & Telemetry Data
* **Device & Sync State**: PWA offline cache state, sync queue version, client IP (hashed for rate limiting), and user-agent metadata.

---

## 3. Learner Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, learners and educators have:
* **Right of Access**: View all stored personal data and academic records directly from the Learner Profile.
* **Right to Rectification**: Update personal details and notification preferences.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Request complete account deletion and purging of uploaded workshop evidence.
* **Right to Data Portability**: Export certified course completion transcripts and badge records in standard JSON/PDF formats.
* **Right to Object / Withdraw Consent**: Switch to offline local guest mode without sharing identifiers with remote servers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing & Transfers

* **No Commercial Monetisation**: Empower never sells, rents, or monetises learner information or browsing behaviors.
* **Accreditation Bodies**: Skills records are shared with national certification bodies (e.g., TVET CDACC, NITA) solely upon explicit learner request for official certification.
